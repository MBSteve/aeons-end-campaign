# Progression Rules

Rules governing mage and team progression throughout the campaign.

---

## Mage Experience (XP)

| Event | XP |
|-------|----|
| Mission completed | 1 XP |
| Victory | +1 XP |
| Bonus objective completed | +1 XP |
| Major heroic achievement | +1 XP (Campaign Master discretion) |

Avoid excessive XP awards.

## Permanent Perks

Permanent perks must be rare. A mage should normally receive **no more than two** permanent perks during the entire campaign.

### Allowed Perk Examples

- Start with one additional charge
- Start with one additional Crystal
- Start with one specific breach focused
- Increase maximum life by 1
- Once per game, gain 1 Aether
- Once per game, focus a breach for 1 less Aether

### Perk Restrictions

- Avoid stacking several economy perks on one mage
- Avoid perks that invalidate a mage's weakness or dominate their identity
- Perks are earned through significant story moments, not routine victories

## Titles

Titles have no mechanical effect unless explicitly stated. They are narrative honours earned through heroic deeds.

### Example Titles

- Defender of the Hollow Mine
- Keeper of the Echo Vault
- The Unbroken
- Warden of the First Gate

## Team Progression

The team may gain:

- **Council Favour** — the main campaign currency
- **Relics** — powerful artifacts with campaign effects
- **Allies** — NPCs who provide benefits
- **Gravehold upgrades** — improvements to the city
- **Access to new locations** — expanded mission options
- **New mission branches** — story forks
- **Intelligence about Nemeses** — tactical advantages
- **Temporary campaign advantages** — one-time benefits

## Council Favour

Council Favour is earned through victories and heroic deeds. It may be spent on:

- Mage training
- Gravehold repairs
- Research
- Relic restoration
- Unlocking side missions
- Recruiting allies
- Improving supplies
- Reducing threat consequences

The cost of each option is stated when it becomes available.
