**CG Artist Reference Notes: "Carapace Queen" from Image**

**Character: Carapace Queen**

*   **Species/Type:** Monstrous, heavily armoured insectoid, resembling a gargantuan praying mantis.
*   **Head:** Segmented, grey-silver metallic carapace. Two large, wicked, serrated scythes form the forearms, held high. Multiple glowing red, faceted eyes on the face. Sharp, segmented mandibles. A small, human-like skull with glowing red eye sockets is embedded at the very top of the head/carapace structure, above the main eyes.
*   **Body:** Covered in thick, segmented, grey-silver articulated plates, suggesting a metal or chitinous material. The plates have subtle brown and dark grey weathering and shading in the recesses. The underbelly and joints are a darker, textured brown, resembling tough leather or unplated chitin. Two large, translucent, veined wings are partially folded against the back, showing a reddish-brown tint.
*   **Glow/Effects:** The eyes on the main face and the skull are a bright, glowing red. Small red lights are present on the smaller "husks" in the background.
*   **Limbs:** Two enormous, raptorial forelegs ending in large, serrated, scythe-like blades, sharp and metallic. Four long, spindly walking legs with pointed tips, armoured with grey-silver segments, support the body.
*   **Pose:** Rearing up, dominant pose. Forelegs raised and spread, ready to strike. Wings slightly unfurled. Standing tall on its four back legs.
*   **Scale:** Dominates the upper half of the card frame. The smaller husks at its feet indicate it is enormous.
