### Strategy Notes

A straightforward, but difficult Nemesis, Umbra Titan has a surprising number of minions, most tailored around the nemesis losing nemesis tokens. Bad luck in turn order rotation can make the fight near-impossible. If you can, include cards in the market that can mess with the turn order deck such as Carbonize . Since even at nemesis Level 1 there will be at least 2 minions it is key to bring mages with good early damage capabilities. With an unlucky turn order rotation Cryptid can be debilitating. Consider bringing mages that can cast spells in their main phase, or help allies prepare spells outside of their turn such as Indira or Mist (War Eternal) . Umbra Titan's Unleash, minions and abilities usually offer the players to take damage instead of Nemesis Token loss, so mages with good healing capabilities are also viable, but be mindful that they too must focus on some early damage to remove minions.
