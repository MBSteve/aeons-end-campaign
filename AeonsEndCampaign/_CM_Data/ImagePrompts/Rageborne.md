## Canonical Facts

- Rageborne is a Nemesis from the Aeon's End core set, Tier 2
- It is a creature of pure destructive instinct — unmatched in strength and determination
- It does not plan, scheme, or command minions — it simply destroys everything in its path
- It is described as "the executioner" — the Nameless's champion, sent to quicken Gravehold's end
- It charges up Fury as it fights, unleashing devastating Strikes
- It has been drawn to Gravehold by the psychic disturbance beneath the city

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- A massive, terrifying creature — chitin, muscle, jagged edges, and wrong angles
- Four or more limbs, each ending in brutal talons or claws
- No visible eyes — or eyes that are dead, black pits reflecting nothing
- A body built for destruction — thick hide, exposed bone ridges, natural armour plating
- It should look like a creature, not a person in a suit — alien, biological, horrifying
- The ash-fields of Gravehold's eastern approach as the setting — grey powder, blackened stone, dawn light

### Prohibited Elements

- No minions, husks, or smaller creatures accompanying it
- No weapons, armour, or crafted items — it is pure creature
- No glowing runes, neon effects, or colourful magic
- No steampunk elements
- No dragon-like features — no wings, no fire breath
- No humanoid face or expression

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: Wide cinematic shot, 16:9. The creature dominates the foreground, mid-stride, advancing across the ash-fields. In the distance, barely visible through atmospheric haze, the silhouette of Gravehold's outer wall and the East Gate. The creature is seen from a low angle, emphasising its size and menace.
- **Mood**: Overwhelming, inevitable, terrifying. The inexorable approach of pure destruction.
- **Lighting**: Low dawn light from the horizon behind the creature, casting it in partial silhouette with rim lighting on its chitin and ridges. The ash-fields are grey and cold. The sky is a muted, bruised grey.
- **Colour Palette**: Ash grey, bone white, dark chitin black, muted rust, pale dawn amber, deep shadow.
- **Art Style**: Photorealistic dark-fantasy cinematic. Like a film still, not a fantasy illustration.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9.
