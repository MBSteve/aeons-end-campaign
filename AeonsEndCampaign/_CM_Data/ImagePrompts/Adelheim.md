## Canonical Facts

- Adelheim is a Breach Mage from the Aeon's End core set
- His starter card is an Amethyst Shard — he channels violet crystal energy
- He is an economy-focused mage who generates Aether and acquires powerful spells
- He is a veteran — confident, composed, has fought Nemeses before
- He was the first mage to arrive at Gravehold when the Council issued the summons

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- An older man, approximately 60–70 years old
- Bald with a neatly trimmed white beard and eyebrows
- Lean, weathered physique rather than heavily muscular
- Practical breach mage attire that also reflects his role as a master weaponsmith
- Subtle violet crystal accents integrated into his equipment
- Calm, analytical expression with the confidence of a veteran who has survived countless battles

### Prohibited Elements

- No glowing runes, neon effects, or colourful magic auras
- No staff, wand, or oversized magical props
- No heroic pose, no smiling
- No steampunk elements, goggles, gears, or Victorian styling
- No young, unlined face
- No glowing white eyes unless specifically required by the scene.

---

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: Full-body standing shot, wide environmental framing. Subject centred, standing in a Gothic stone chamber or forge, facing slightly left as if assessing a distant threat. The surrounding environment — anvil, forge, crystalline tools — visible in the frame. Full figure from head to boots.
- **Mood**: Calm, composed, quietly formidable. The stillness before action.
- **Lighting**: Warm amber light from one side (candlelight or low torchlight), cool shadow on the opposite side. Subtle violet catchlight on one cheek from the amethyst shard.
- **Colour Palette**: Muted charcoal, deep navy, weathered leather brown, subtle violet accent.
- **Art Style**: Photorealistic dark-fantasy cinematic. Like a film still, not a fantasy illustration.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait.
