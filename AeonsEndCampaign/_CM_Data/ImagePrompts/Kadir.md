## Canonical Facts

- Kadir is a Breach Mage from the Aeon's End core set
- Her starter card is an Emerald Shard — she channels green crystal energy
- She is a flexible mage balancing economy with damage output
- She can return spells to hand — a tactician who recycles power
- She arrived at Gravehold through the Warrens, without announcement, as if she had always been there
- She is watchful, understated, and moves with quiet purpose

---

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- A woman in her 30s, watchful and self-contained, with a sharp, assessing gaze
- Practical, understated mage attire — dark tunic, wrapped layers, no ornamentation
- Subtle emerald-green crystal accents — a shard at her wrist or woven into her belt
- Dark hair, practical cut, perhaps braided close to the head
- A face that reveals nothing — guarded, calculating, patient
- A posture suggesting readiness — not tense, but coiled

### Prohibited Elements

- No glowing runes, neon effects, or colourful magic auras
- No staff, wand, or oversized magical props
- No heroic pose, no smiling
- No steampunk elements, goggles, gears, or Victorian styling
- No sexualised costume
- No expression of open emotion — she is guarded

---

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: 85mm portrait, head-and-shoulders. Subject slightly off-centre, looking directly at the viewer with a calm, unreadable expression. Soft blurred stone passageway (the Warrens) behind her.
- **Mood**: Watchful, patient, quietly dangerous. Still waters.
- **Lighting**: Low, directional light from a single source (torch or lantern) casting sharp shadows. Half her face in shadow. Subtle emerald catchlight in one eye.
- **Colour Palette**: Charcoal, dark green, deep brown, slate grey, subtle emerald accent.
- **Art Style**: Photorealistic dark-fantasy cinematic. Like a film still, not a fantasy illustration.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait.
