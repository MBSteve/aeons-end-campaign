### Overview

Reeve is an offensive mage focused on killing minions, with her ability Quelling Blade able to deal a total of 8 damage spread across two minions, and costing only 4 charges. Obsidian Shard can produce 3 , allowing Reeve an economy swing early in the game, if she is willing to take the damage. Reeve also starts with two breaches that are close to open, making an open level 4 breach fairly easy to get.
### Ability

Spend 4 charges to activate this ability during your main phase: Deal 5 damage to a minion. Deal 3 damage to a different minion.
### Strategy Notes

If there's a 6 cost in the market that really interests you, you can purchase it without giving up your breach development and draw it on turn 4. Just open your II breach turn 1, cast only one spark on turn 2 and focus your III breach, then you'll draw into Obsidian Shard, 3 crystals, and a spark. Cast two of your sparks, and you'll be able to draw straight into that 6-cost card on turn 4.
