## Canonical Facts

Gravehold is humanity's last great city.

It sits high within a mountain valley.

The city is heavily fortified.

Most of the population lives above ground.

The mountain contains extensive underground districts, but these are NOT shown in this map.

## Required Elements

Show:

A detailed medieval fantasy city enclosed by enormous stone walls.

The Spire dominates the city centre.

The Upper Ward surrounds the Spire.

The Lower Markets occupy much of the lower city.

Multiple gates.

Stone roads.

Dense medieval buildings.

Outer settlements outside the walls.

Farmland.

Mountain forests.

Cliffs.

Mountain passes leading toward unknown lands.

The city should feel like humanity's final refuge.
