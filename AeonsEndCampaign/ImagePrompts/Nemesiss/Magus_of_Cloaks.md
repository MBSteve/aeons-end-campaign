### Strategy Notes

(This guide was primarily written with 2 players in mind, other player counts might necessitate different strategies) Magus of Cloaks is a tricky boss, as you need both numerous cheap spells and big damage ones at the same time. You cannot ignore hitting this Nameless like some others, its Nemesis-specific minions are designed to be very powerful if Magus of Cloaks has lots of Nemesis Tokens. Garu is a very strong contender for this one, as he can essentially turn cheap spells into a big, nasty nuke. In fact, pretty much only mages like Garu have the penetrative power to beat this Nemesis before the Nemesis Deck runs dry. A passive outlasting cheese strategy, which is viable against say, Hollow Crown is not viable here. Ashen Haruspex and Veil Daughter have enough damage and resilience that a passive healing setup is not viable. In addition, Shadow's Reach starts a countdown, so the players winning by the Nemesis running out of cards is not possible. Consider specializing mages: one mage focuses on big boom spells, the other one focuses on many small ones. Turn order rotation randomness might make this risky though. Know the Nemesis Deck: I know, we love surprises, but not knowing what to expect may spell doom here. Well, you are reading this so you might have already failed once or twice ;). There are 3 Nemesis cards that immediately grant Magus of Cloaks a LOT of Nemesis Tokens. One of these is a Tier 2 card. So, there is no point attempting to hit the Nemesis knowing that a card like that is incoming soon. Say, with 2 players, out of the 5 Tier 2 Nemesis cards 4 have already been played, so the 5th must be Enshroud . In that case you have decisions to make: If the Nemesis has few Nemesis Tokens, try to hit it with everything you have, to squeeze in as much damage as possible before its 'shields' go up again. If the Nemesis has a lot of Nemesis Tokens, then ignore the Nemesis and hit minions, cancel Power cards, optimize your deck etc. If the Nemesis already has 8 Nemesis Tokens, who cares if it gains an additional 4, it can only have 8 at maximum anyway. Luck plays a large role unfortunately. An early Veil Daughter followed by Shadow's Reach and Eclipse will most likely be run ending, especially with 2 players. If you can beat Veil Daughter before these come into play, or if Veil Daughter comes up after these cards are out, then you have a chance.
