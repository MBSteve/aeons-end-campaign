## Canonical Facts

- Kellan Voss is a third-generation miner from the Deepvein crew
- Human man
- Sole survivor of the Deepvein disappearance — missing for eleven days
- Crawled out of a ventilation shaft near the Old Foundry
- Emaciated, dehydrated, covered in a fine grey dust that healers could not identify
- Eyes have gone the colour of tarnished silver
- Clutches a black crystal in his right hand — no larger than a child's fist, pulsing with a light that drinks torchlight rather than reflecting it
- Healers could not remove the crystal from his hand; he screamed when they tried
- Currently unconscious in the Healer's Ward
- Final words: "It doesn't kill. It waits."
- Said the others are "still down there, in a way"

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- An emaciated, gaunt human man lying on a cot or bed in a healer's ward
- Skin pale and dusted with fine grey powder
- Eyes visible — tarnished silver colour, either open and vacant or closed with a suggestion of wrongness
- Right hand visible, clutching a small black crystal that pulses with a sickly black radiance — the crystal should appear to absorb surrounding light rather than emit it
- Healer's ward setting — simple cot, perhaps a ward-lantern nearby, stone walls
- Signs of dehydration and prolonged absence (sunken cheeks, cracked lips)

### Prohibited Elements

- No visible wounds, blood, or gore
- No monster or entity visible in the scene
- No glowing heroic or holy light
- No peaceful or restful expression — he should look disturbed even in unconsciousness
- No other characters in the scene (the focus is on Kellan alone)

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: Medium shot from beside or slightly above the cot; Kellan fills most of the frame, the black crystal prominent in the foreground or mid-ground; the healer's ward recedes into shadow behind him
- **Mood**: Unsettling, quiet horror, wrongness barely contained
- **Lighting**: Dramatic chiaroscuro — warm torchlight from a single ward-lantern or candle, casting deep shadows; the light should feel like it's being pulled toward the crystal — the crystal is the darkest point in the image yet paradoxically the focal point; shadows pool unnaturally around the crystal. Cool ambient shadows, subtle atmospheric haze
- **Colour Palette**: Muted earth tones — sepia, charcoal grey, tarnished silver, deep browns; the crystal is a void-black that seems to dim the colours around it; no warm tones except the lantern light; no saturated colours
- **Art Style**: Cinematic dark-fantasy realism. Photorealistic high-end fantasy game cinematic. Grounded medieval Gothic. Kellan should look like a real person — gaunt, dehydrated, disturbed — with realistic skin pores, cracked lips, individual hairs, and the fine grey dust visibly coating his skin. The crystal should look like a real physical object. NOT illustrated, NOT painted, NOT hand-drawn. The wrongness should be conveyed through lighting, expression, and the crystal's unnatural light-absorbing quality
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
