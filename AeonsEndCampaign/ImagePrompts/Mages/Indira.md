### Overview

During her Apprentice ordeal, Indira was attacked by a Needlemaw after falling into a chasm full of glyph covered columns. She fought off the Needlemaw but was knocked unconscious by falling rock. Yan Magda and Garu found and returned her to Gravehold. As Indira never formally completed her training, she is not a true breach mage; this is reflected by her having only 1 breach available to her. However, Indira is still an offensive mage; her ability Pyromancer's Guile and two copies of her unique Twin Opal allow her to cast spells directly from her hand. This makes Indira capable of reacting quickly, casting spells the same turn she drew them.
### Ability

Spend 4 charges to activate this ability during your main phase: Cast any number of spells in hand. Those spells each deal 1 additional damage. You may destroy a card in your discard pile.
### Strategy Notes

Perhaps the most important action you can take as Indira is finding a way to free up her Twin Opals so they don't have to cast Sparks all day. The two main ways to do this is to either destroy her sparks or open her III breach, so try and make a plan to do one of those.
