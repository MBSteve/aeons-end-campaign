# Image Prompt: Gravehold Underground Cross-Section

---

> **Style Guide**: Hand-painted dark fantasy. Ink linework with subtle watercolour. Oblique bird's-eye or cinematic perspective. Palette: muted earth tones, slate grey, weathered stone, forest green, ochre — no saturated colours. Lighting: natural, overcast skies, soft sunlight, warm torchlight indoors. Avoid: anime, steampunk, photorealism, concept art, comic book, 3D render, digital painting, video game UI. Overall feeling: a forgotten medieval world documented by a master cartographer.

---

## Asset Information

Gravehold Underground Cross-Section v1

Purpose:

## Canonical Facts

This is NOT a city map.

It is an architectural cutaway showing the underground infrastructure beneath Gravehold.

The surface city should only be visible as a thin strip across the top.

The focus is the underground.

## Required Features

Large crystal veins.

Mining tunnels.

Ancient support columns.

Stone lifts.

Vertical shafts.

Reservoirs.

Collapsed passages.

Ancient abandoned chambers.

Natural caverns.

Huge carved halls.

## Artistic Direction

Composition:

Vertical cutaway through the mountain.

The viewer should immediately understand how enormous the underground city is.

Mood:

Ancient.

Oppressive.

Claustrophobic.

Lighting:

Torchlight.

Dim crystal glow.

Almost complete darkness in abandoned regions.

Colour Palette:

Dark stone.

Slate grey.

Amber torchlight.

Cold blue crystal light.

Style:

Architectural fantasy cutaway.

Detailed enough that the viewer could imagine travelling through the tunnels.

Inspired by archaeological reconstruction artwork rather than engineering blueprints.
