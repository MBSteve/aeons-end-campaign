## Canonical Facts

- Brama is a Breach Mage from the Aeon's End core set
- Her starter card is Buried Light — she channels restorative, gentle energy
- She is a support/healer mage — healing, deck manipulation, sustain
- She was tending a fever outbreak in the Outer Settlements when the summons came

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- A woman in her 30s–40s, warm but tired, with the steady gaze of someone who has seen too much suffering
- Practical healer's attire — simple tunic, shawl or wrap, a satchel of tinctures visible
- Subtle warm golden-amber light emanating from one hand or a small crystal at her collar
- Hair tied back practically, perhaps with a few loose strands
- Hands that show work — a healer's hands
- A face marked by compassion, not hardness

### Prohibited Elements

- No glowing runes, neon effects, or colourful magic auras
- No staff, wand, or oversized magical props
- No heroic pose, no smiling
- No steampunk elements, goggles, gears, or Victorian styling
- No pristine, unweathered clothing
- No sexualised costume

---

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: 85mm portrait, three-quarter. Subject centred, looking slightly upward and to the right as if toward a patient or a distant hope. Soft blurred Gothic archway or infirmary wall behind her.
- **Mood**: Warm, steady, weary but unbroken. The quiet strength of a healer in a world of war.
- **Lighting**: Warm golden light (candlelight or low hearth-fire) from below-right, casting soft shadows across her face. Cool ambient grey filling the shadows.
- **Colour Palette**: Muted earth tones, warm ochre, deep brown, soft cream, subtle amber accent.
- **Art Style**: Photorealistic dark-fantasy cinematic. Like a film still, not a fantasy illustration.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait.
