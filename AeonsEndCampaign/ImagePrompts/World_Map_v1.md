# Image Prompt: World Map

---

> **Style Guide**: Hand-painted dark fantasy. Ink linework with subtle watercolour. Oblique bird's-eye or cinematic perspective. Palette: muted earth tones, slate grey, weathered stone, forest green, ochre — no saturated colours. Lighting: natural, overcast skies, soft sunlight, warm torchlight indoors. Avoid: anime, steampunk, photorealism, concept art, comic book, 3D render, digital painting, video game UI. Overall feeling: a forgotten medieval world documented by a master cartographer.

---

## Asset Information

- **Asset Name**: World Map v1
- **Purpose**: Campaign world map for `Maps/` directory; establishes the geography of the known world
- **Intended Repository Path**: `Maps/World_v1.png`

---

## Canonical Facts

- Gravehold is the last great city of humanity, built into and beneath a mountain range
- The world is dead or dying — this is a post-cataclysm setting
- Gravehold has surface fortifications, mid-level habitation zones, and deep subterranean works
- Outer Settlements exist as satellite communities beyond the walls
- Ash-hawks migrate through the skies — a native species suggesting volcanic or ash-plain terrain
- The world has experienced multiple Nemesis assaults over a generation
- Known locations: Deepvein Tunnels (eastern mining, sealed), Silver Rift (western mining, sealed), Old Foundry
- The setting is dark fantasy — not a lush or vibrant world

---

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- A mountain range dominating the central region — Gravehold should be indicated as a fortified city carved into these mountains
- Harsh, unforgiving terrain surrounding the mountains — ash plains, dead forests, broken badlands, or crystalline wastelands
- At least one or two Outer Settlements marked as small nodes beyond the city
- The general sense of a world that has been scarred by repeated cataclysms
- Subtle markers or labels for key known features (The Spire, the sealed tunnels, Outer Settlements)
- A dark, atmospheric colour palette consistent with the campaign tone

### Prohibited Elements

- No vibrant green forests, blue oceans, or lush farmland — this is not a fertile world
- No major cities other than Gravehold
- No visible Nemesis or monster forms on the map
- No clearly defined continent shapes suggesting a traditional fantasy world — the map should feel partial, as if the known world is limited
- No cheerful cartouches, sea monsters, or whimsical map decorations

---

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: Top-down or slightly angled cartographic view; Gravehold at the centre, terrain radiating outward; the edges of the map should fade into unknown territory — fog, darkness, or simply unmapped blankness
- **Mood**: Bleak, ancient, and isolated — this is the last refuge in a dying world
- **Lighting**: Flat, overcast lighting suggesting a sunless or ash-choked sky; no dramatic directional light
- **Colour Palette**: Ashen greys, burnt umbers, muted ochres, deep charcoal; the mountain range in dark slate and iron grey; any crystal deposits marked in faint, sickly pale-blue or amber
- **Art Style**: Dark fantasy cartography — painterly and textured, like an in-world map drawn on aged parchment or vellum by a Gravehold cartographer; not a satellite-style map
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
