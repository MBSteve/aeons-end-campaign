## Canonical Facts

- Xaxos is a Breach Mage from the Aeon's End core set
- His starter card is Flare — he channels intense, fiery energy
- He is a spell-focused mage — charges breaches, casts often, delivers raw magical power
- His breaches were already faintly aglow when he arrived at Gravehold
- He is intense, passionate, and carries a restless energy — the air hums around him

---

## Visual Requirements

### Required Elements

- A man in his late 20s–30s, intense and restless, with the look of someone holding back a storm
- Practical mage attire — dark tunic, perhaps singed at the edges, sleeves rolled back
- Subtle warm orange/amber glow at his hands or leaking from his eyes — restrained, not blazing
- Dark hair, slightly dishevelled, as if he's been channelling power for hours
- A face that burns with conviction — not angry, but driven
- The air around him should feel charged — subtle heat-haze distortion at the edges

### Prohibited Elements

- No glowing runes, neon effects, or colourful magic auras
- No staff, wand, or oversized magical props
- No heroic pose, no smiling
- No steampunk elements, goggles, gears, or Victorian styling
- No full-body flames or fireballs — power is contained, not released
- No angry or snarling expression

---

## Artistic Direction

- **Composition**: 85mm portrait, three-quarter. Subject centred, slightly angled, looking past the viewer with fierce focus. Soft blurred stone archway behind him. Subtle heat-haze effect at the edges of the frame.
- **Mood**: Intense, contained, volatile. The calm before the fire.
- **Lighting**: Warm amber/orange light from the subject himself (his hands or breaches), casting upward shadows on his face. Cool ambient darkness around him.
- **Colour Palette**: Charcoal, deep rust, burnt orange, warm amber, dark brown.
- **Art Style**: Photorealistic dark-fantasy cinematic. Like a film still, not a fantasy illustration.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait.
