**Character Subject: The Remnant (Aethereal Entity)**

**General Composition and Build:**
The entity is a non-biological, magical construct shown from the mid-torso up. It possesses no flesh, blood, or bone. Its form is comprised of a turbulent, swirling mass of arcane energy that holds together ancient armored components. The general shape suggests a humanoid upper torso and head, but without defined arms, abdomen, or neck. It appears to be floating or suspended.

**Head (The Mask):**
*   **Type:** The "face" is an inorganic mask, detached from any underlying biology.

The surface is heavily weathered with fine and deep structural cracks running across the forehead, down the left cheek, over the nose bridge, and across the chin.
*   **Colour:** Off-white, aged ivory, with deeper grey/brown tones settled into the cracks and recesses.

*   **Integration:** The mask is cradled within the neck area of the energy body, with the blue arcane energy eddying around its edges and slightly obscuring the very bottom of the chin.

*   **Material/Effect:** The main body is composed of intense, volatile magical energy. It should look like a cross between swirling gaseous smoke, liquid fire, and plasma. It does not have a solid surface; it is translucent in areas and opaque in others due to density.
*   **Colour Palette:** A deep, rich spectrum of blues. It ranges from near-black indigo in the deepest recesses and shadows, transitioning through vibrant cobalt, to intense electric cyan and bright white-blue at the edges and highest energy points.
*   **Motion & Texture:** The energy must look dynamic and turbulent, churning upwards in flame-like wisps and tendrils. It has an internal luminescence that casts light on the armor pieces. The edges are undefined and vaporous.
