**Character Reference:** Umbra Titan

**1. Overall Subject:**
The character is a massive, segmented, worm-like monstrosity, shown rearing up from a subterranean environment. The image is composed on a card-like layout with a large text box overlaid on the lower half of the body.

**2. Anatomy & Structure:**
*   **Body Type:** Segmented giant earthworm-like form.
*   **Segments:** Numerous, distinct, cylindrical segments. Each segment is covered in thick, chitinous plates with a rock-like, weathered texture.
*   **Head/Mouth:** The creature has no face. Its anterior end opens into a massive, glowing, star-shaped maw. This maw is formed by numerous pointed, fleshy projections that radiate outwards. These projections are dark brown with lighter, spotted patterns.
*   **Internal Glow:** Intense, bright yellow-green light emanates from the center of the maw and from deep within the gaps and under the edges of the body segments.
