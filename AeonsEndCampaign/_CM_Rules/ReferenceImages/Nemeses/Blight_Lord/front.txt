Here is a detailed reference description of the "Blight Lord" for a CGI artist:

**Subject:** Blight Lord
**Type:** Monstrous humanoid-scorpion hybrid.
**Overall Impression:** Massive, imposing creature of dark, segmented armor and raw, glowing green crystals.

**1. Anatomy & Structure**
*   **Worm/Scorpion Lower Body:** The lower half is a large, multi-legged arthropod body, similar to a scorpion or centipede.
    *   **Legs:** Multiple pairs of thick, segmented, dark brown/bronze legs with sharp, clawed feet. The legs have a chitinous, armored texture.
    *   **Tail:** A thick, armored tail curves up and forward, ending in a large, curved sting heavily integrated with green crystals.
*   **Humanoid Upper Body:** A powerful, heavily armored humanoid torso rises from the arachnid base.
    *   **Head/Helm:** The head is a dark, angular, monstrous helm with sharp plates. Jagged green crystals erupt from the top and back of the head, forming a crown/mane. The face is obscured, with a glowing green area suggesting eyes or a visor.
    *   **Torso:** Broad, segmented dark armor plates cover the chest and abdomen. A cluster of sharp green crystals juts out from the center of the chest.
    *   **This is a monster, not a human in a suit, so the armor is its carapace.**

**2. Arms & Weapons (Mutated)**
*   **Right Arm:** This arm is powerful and armored, ending in a clawed hand with large, sharp fingers. Green crystals are embedded along the forearm and back of the hand. The hand is open, possibly gesturing or reaching.
*   **Left Arm:** The entire left forearm and hand are mutated into a massive, oversized pincer claw, similar to a scorpion's or crab's. The claw is constructed from large, jagged green crystals that glow intensely. Smaller crystal formations sprout from the armored shoulder and upper arm.

**3. Dominant Feature: Green Crystals**
*   **Location:** Raw, jagged formations of emerald-green crystals burst from the character's back, shoulders, chest, head/helm, right arm, left pincer-claw, and tail stinger.
*   **Appearance:** The crystals are sharp, unpolished, and appear to be growing aggressively out of the armor. They should look like raw minerals, not cut gems.
*   **Glow:** All crystals emit a strong, internal emerald-green glow, serving as the primary light source for the character and immediate surroundings.

**4. Color & Texture Palette**
*   **Armor/Carapace:** Deep, dark earth tones (bronze, dark brown, charcoal black). The texture is hard, segmented, and chitinous, with signs of wear, scratches, and dents. The metal/shell appears ancient and heavy.
*   **Crystals:** Intense, glowing emerald green. The material is translucent yet solid, with internal fractures and a crystalline structure that catches and radiates light.
*   **Underlying Body:** Where armor plates meet or in joints, a darker, perhaps leathery or shadowed flesh might be suggested, but it is primarily an armored creature.

**5. Pose & Composition**
*   **Pose:** The Blight Lord is in a dynamic, threatening pose. The body is angled slightly to the viewer's left. The large left crystal pincer is brought forward, dominating the foreground. The right arm is held out to the side. The tail curves over the back, ready to strike.
*   **Framing:** This is a three-quarter portrait shot, showing most of the creature from the mid-legs up. It is framed within an ornate, mossy, root-covered border.

**6. Environment & Lighting**
*   **Background:** A dark, subterranean cavern or alien landscape. Sharp, green crystal formations stud the ground and form the blurry background wall. The ground is uneven and rocky. The background is a wash of dark greens, greys, and blacks.
*   **Lighting:** The primary light source is the character's own glowing crystals. This creates dramatic, high-contrast lighting. Bright green light casts sharp highlights on the edges of the armor and crystals, while deep, dark shadows fill the recesses, emphasizing the creature's menacing form. The glow illuminates the immediate rocky ground.
