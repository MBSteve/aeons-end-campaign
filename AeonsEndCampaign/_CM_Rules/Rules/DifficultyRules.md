# Difficulty Rules

Rules governing campaign difficulty for experienced players.

---

## Difficulty Tracking

The Campaign Master tracks:

- Consecutive victories
- Consecutive defeats
- Average remaining Gravehold life
- Number of exhausted mages
- Nemesis tier
- Market strength
- Persistent advantages
- Persistent penalties

## Difficulty Adjustment

Difficulty should increase gradually. Do not increase difficulty only by adding health.

### Preferred Methods

- More demanding Nemeses
- Less synergistic markets
- Risk-reward bonus objectives
- Persistent mission pressure
- Harder branching choices
- Limited pre-game penalties
- Consequences from prior failures

### Restrictions

- Avoid making a mission unwinnable because of accumulated penalties
- Do not create difficulty through arbitrary punishment
- Ensure player skill and choices remain the primary factor in outcomes
