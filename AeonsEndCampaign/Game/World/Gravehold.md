Gravehold is not merely a city — it is the last refuge. Built into and beneath a mountain range, its districts span surface fortifications, mid-level habitation zones, and deep subterranean works. The city is powered by crystal veins that run through the bedrock, harvested and refined by generations of miners and artificers.

The Breach Mages are Gravehold's primary defence. These spellcasters channel power through breaches — unstable rifts in reality — to fight the Nameless, monstrous entities that periodically assault the city. The Council governs Gravehold, composed of elder mages, district representatives, and military commanders.

### Known Districts

- **The Spire** — Council chambers and administrative heart
- **Upper Ward** — Residential district for mages and officials
- **Lower Markets** — Trade, commerce, and common housing
- **The Foundry** — Industrial district, smithing, and crystal refinement
- **Old Foundry** — Abandoned industrial sector, partially sealed
